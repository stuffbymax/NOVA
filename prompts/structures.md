# Buildings, stations, and megastructures

Create an original concept-art sheet for [STRUCTURE NAME], a [STRUCTURE TYPE]
used by the [CIVILIZATION NAME]. Show a planetary building or orbital
station in three-quarter view, an interior cutaway, and a small silhouette
comparison. Include [FUNCTION], [POWER SOURCE], [DEFENSES], inhabitants or
workers, docking or transit areas, and signs of local materials.

Structure type: colony habitat, capital city, spaceport, research lab,
shipyard, defense platform, mining complex, temple, megastructure, or ruined
precursor site. Make it practical, visually distinct, and suitable for a
strategy-game build menu asset.
