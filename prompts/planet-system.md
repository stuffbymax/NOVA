# Planet system

Square transparent-background game asset showing the [STAR TYPE] star and
[NUMBER] orbiting planets in a readable angled diagram. Include varied planet
sizes, atmospheric colors, a rocky [SPECIAL WORLD], and a thin asteroid belt.
Choose original planet classes from continental, ocean, desert, arctic, jungle,
volcanic, toxic, barren, ash, tomb, gas giant, ring world, artificial habitat,
or shattered world. Add one moon, one space station, and subtle orbital
markers without readable text.

Keep clear separation between bodies and leave generous transparent padding so
the system can be placed on a map. Make every planet visually distinct enough
to communicate its class at a glance while preserving a coherent star system.
