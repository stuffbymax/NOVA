# Anomalies and exploration discoveries

Create an atmospheric exploration-discovery illustration for [ANOMALY NAME]:
a [ANOMALY TYPE] found near [LOCATION]. Show the discovery from a survey
ship's perspective and include a small scale reference such as a ship, probe,
or planet. Communicate [SCIENTIFIC MYSTERY], [DANGER], and [POSSIBLE REWARD]
through composition, lighting, and environmental details.

Anomaly types may include a living nebula, rogue planet, artificial moon,
time distortion, impossible signal, space leviathan, ancient gateway, hidden
colony, dimensional fracture, or stellar remnant. Keep the mystery original
and avoid horror gore or recognizable franchise creatures.

Append the shared style in [visual-style.md](visual-style.md).
